# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Contributors should assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback,
respect different backgrounds and experience levels, and focus disagreement on
the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according
to the seriousness and pattern of behavior. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
